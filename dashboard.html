FAQs

How do I reset my PIN?
Go to Settings → Security → Change PIN. You'll need to verify via OTP sent to your registered phone number.
How long do transfers take?
Intra-bank transfers are instant. Inter-bank (NIBSS) transfers typically complete within 30 seconds to 5 minutes.
What are my daily transaction limits?
Daily transfer: ₦5,000,000. POS: ₦500,000. ATM: ₦150,000. Increase limits via Cards → Limits.
How do I block my card?
Navigate to Cards, select the card, and tap Block. You can also call our 24/7 hotline: 0800-ANTIGRAVITY.
How secure is Antigravity Bank?
We use 256-bit AES encryption, 2FA, and biometric authentication. All data is stored on CBN-regulated servers with continuous fraud monitoring.
